## 5. Aizen Raid Rules

These rules apply whenever you join, attempt to join, or participate in a raid hosted through **Aizen Suske** or another XGC raid bot.

### 5.1 Joining a Lobby and Backing Out

Joining an XGC-hosted raid lobby and backing out without a valid reason is prohibited.

Backing out after joining may force the host bot to complete the raid with too few players or alone, which can substantially increase completion time and consume system, network, subscription, and moderation resources. Raid participants are warned of this rule in advance.

XGC may use automated alerts or detection systems to identify possible lobby departures. Enforcement decisions are reviewed by staff before a punishment is applied when reasonably practical.

If you leave due to a genuine mistake, technical issue, emergency, or other valid reason, contact XGC Staff promptly through the designated support channel or direct message.

Possible consequences include:

- Warning
- Temporary suspension from raids or bot access
- Suspension for up to 48 hours
- Longer suspension or permanent ban for repeated, intentional, or disruptive conduct

### 5.2 Trolling, Throwing, and Disruptive Raid Conduct

Do not intentionally sabotage, troll, throw, delay, grief, or otherwise interfere with an XGC-hosted raid.

Examples may include intentionally using actions designed to prevent raid completion, repeatedly joining and leaving, knowingly disrupting coordinated strategies, or engaging in behavior intended to waste the time of the host or other participants.

Intentional sabotage may result in an immediate suspension or permanent ban from XGC Bots and/or the XGC server.

### 5.3 Trainer Names and Pokémon Nicknames

Trainer names, IGN values, Pokémon nicknames, and other in-game identifiers must comply with XGC rules and Discord policies.

The following are prohibited:

- Names that advertise websites, services, marketplaces, or Pokémon-selling services.
- Names containing sexually explicit, hateful, discriminatory, abusive, or otherwise inappropriate content.
- Names intended to evade moderation or promote prohibited content.
- Names that reasonably appear to advertise a `.com` Pokémon seller or similar commercial service.

XGC may issue a warning or temporary suspension for a first offense where appropriate. Repeated violations, serious misconduct, or commercial advertising may result in a permanent ban.

### 5.4 IGN Identification Requirement

Your IGN must appear in your Discord display name, server nickname, profile, registration record, or another clear method that reasonably links your Discord identity to your in-game identity.

This requirement allows XGC Staff to identify participants quickly when a raid, trade, queue, moderation issue, or support request requires follow-up.

If XGC Staff cannot reasonably identify you from your IGN, you may be temporarily suspended or otherwise restricted until your identity can be confirmed. Repeated refusal to identify your IGN may result in a longer restriction or ban.
